# Paper Instructions

- Overleaf is authoritative for prose, bibliography, and hand-edited figures.
- Pull before editing locally, building, freezing, or pushing generated assets.
- Local outbound sync is limited to bootstrap and `generated/` unless an
  explicit recovery procedure is documented.
- Every quantitative figure, table, and major claim must have an entry in
  `evidence.yaml` with resolvable experiment IDs.
- Do not expose author identities or private repository URLs during double blind.
